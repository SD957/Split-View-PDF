On the first day of spring, the Hillview Public Library reopened after a year of
repairs. Volunteers repainted the reading room, restored the oak tables, and
returned a small archive of letters written by local farmers in the 1930s.

During the opening ceremony, the director thanked neighbors for donating tools and
tea. Children gathered around a display of weather journals, while retired
teachers described the library as a quiet place where the town remembers itself.
